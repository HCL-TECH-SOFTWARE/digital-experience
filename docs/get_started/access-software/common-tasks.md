# My HCLSoftware (MHS) Delivery Portal Account Management

## Checking your Entitlements in the My HCLSoftware portal

Please visit the HCL Support Knowledge Article KB0120188 [How to Manage account membership in My HCLSoftware portal (MHS)](https://support.hcl-software.com/csm?id=kb_article&sysparm_article=KB0120188) to for instructions to view the software entitlements and associated maintenance for your organization.
