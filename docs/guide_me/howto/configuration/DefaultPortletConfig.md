# How to change the default Portlet configuration

## Applies to

> HCL Digital Experience v9.5 and higher

## Introduction

This article describes how to change the default settings of a Portlet on all of the pages where it's deployed.

???+ info "Important"
    If the change is made to a Portlet located in the base Portal, it will take affect on the deployments of the Portlet in all virtual Portals as well. If the change is made to a Portlet in a Virtual Portal, it will take affect on the deployments of the Portlet in all Virtual Portals as well as the base Portal.

## Instructions

To change the default settings of a Portlet, do the following:

1. Login to Portal as an administrator.
2. Navigate to a page where the portlet in question is deployed.
3. Turn on **Edit Mode**.
4. Locate the Portlet that you want to modify on the page.
5. Click on the menu icon in the upper right hand corner of the Portlet
6. Click on **Configure**.
7. Locate the setting that you want to modify, and make the required change.
8. Click on **Apply** to save your changes.
