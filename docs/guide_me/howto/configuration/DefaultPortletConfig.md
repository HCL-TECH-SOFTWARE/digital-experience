# How to Change the Default Portlet Configuration

## Applies to

> HCL Digital Experience v9.5 and Higher

## Introduction

This knowledge article describes how to change the default settings of a portlet on all of the pages where it's deployed.

!!!note "IMPORTANT"
    If the change is made to a portlet in the base portal, it will take affect on the deployments of the portlet in all virtual portals as well.  If the change is made to a portlet in a virtual portal, it will take affect on the deployments of the portlet in all virtual portals as well as the base portal.

## Instructions

To change the default settings of a portlet, do the following:

1. Login to Portal as an Administrator.
2. Go to a page where the portlet in question is deployed.
3. Turn on Edit Mode.
4. Locate the portlet that you want to modify on the page.
5. Click on the menu icon in the upper righthand corner of the portlet
6. Click on "Configure".
7. Locate the setting that you want to modify, and make the required change.
8. Click on apply to save your changes.
