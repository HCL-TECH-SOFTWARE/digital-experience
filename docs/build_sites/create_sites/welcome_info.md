# Information and Education \| HCL Digital Experience

Formal and informal education, documentation, and community content are available to help you get started and achieve your goals.

## Information mode

Information mode displays additional inline information, examples, and hover help in the user interface, such as the site toolbar. It provides extra guidance when needed. Once you are familiar with the interface, you can turn off Information mode.

Information mode is a global setting. From the site toolbar, you can turn it on or off to suit your needs.

Information mode is not implemented across the entire user interface. For example, it is not available in the administration portlets. If you have feedback or suggestions about Information mode, leave a comment.

## User assistance help system

The help system is designed to answer questions and clarify the user interface. More detailed information is available in the product documentation. It also links to the product documentation for additional guidance.

The help system includes two plug-ins (books): one for administrators and one for content authors.

- The administrator book provides information to support the administration portlets.
- The content authors book helps content authors get the most out of the site toolbar. It also includes overview information for users who are new to HCL Web Content Manager.

## HCL Digital Experience product documentation

HCL Web Content Manager and HCL Portal are tightly integrated. To reflect this integration, their documentation is delivered as a single content collection in HCL Digital Experience product documentation, providing a unified source of information.

To support teams and administrators, the product documentation includes the help system books. You cannot edit content in HCL Digital Experience product documentation, but you can provide feedback by adding comments.
